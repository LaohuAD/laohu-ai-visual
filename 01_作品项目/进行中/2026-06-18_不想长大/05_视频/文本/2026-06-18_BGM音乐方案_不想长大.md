# 《不想长大》短剧 BGM 音乐生成方案

## 01_怎么用

音乐生成工具里通常有两个关键输入框：

```text
Style Prompt / 风格提示词：填声音风格、BPM、配器、空间、情绪。
Lyrics / Custom Lyrics / 歌词：纯 BGM 时不填歌词正文，而是填曲式结构和分段控制。
```

所以，纯 BGM 不是“歌词框空着”。歌词框要填类似下面这种结构：

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ solo felt piano ｜ old room tone]
[Theme A ｜ music box motif ｜ soft kalimba]
[Build ｜ low strings enter ｜ tension tightens]
[Break ｜ sudden silence ｜ leave room for dialogue]
[Outro ｜ final note fades ｜ 2 seconds air]
```

这样模型才知道这段音乐怎么起、怎么变、哪里停、哪里给对白让空间。上一版只给了风格提示词，少了这个结构控制，所以不够能用。

## 02_整片统一声音规则

这部短剧不适合用一首煽情歌从头铺到底。它真正的痛点不是“哭”，而是小孩当下不懂，观众先替他懂。所以 BGM 要分成几条短 cue，前半段保留童年烟火气，后半段逐渐把热闹抽空，最后只剩木车轮子、旧话回响和迟来的酸。

统一母题：

```text
三音木车母题：music box / kalimba / muted piano，像木轮咕噜转一下。
前半段明亮一点，后半段同一个母题放慢、变空、变冷。
```

统一制作边界：

```text
纯器乐 BGM。
人声位置留给剧中对白和 VO。
鼓点轻，动态克制，情绪不直接按哭。
丧葬段做空、慢、迟钝，不做恐怖。
结尾保留环境声和尾音，不要把音乐填满。
```

## 03_优先生成顺序

如果先做小样，建议先生成三条：

```text
1. 两辆完整木车和半成品车架：结尾主情绪，决定全片是否成立。
2. 爷爷的木车：前半段温暖和童年快乐。
3. 汤圆硬币：中段冲突和静音断点。
```

完整剪辑再补：

```text
4. 不情愿回老家
5. 病床和不懂死亡
6. 爷爷走了
7. 奶奶的叮嘱到遗像硬切
```

## 04_Cue 1：不情愿回老家

用途：城市卧室、车上、乡路过渡。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental nostalgic childhood film cue, 76 BPM, 4/4, soft felt piano, muted music box accents, light nylon guitar harmonics, gentle brushed percussion, warm morning room tone, subtle countryside transition, child POV, innocent stubborn mood, intimate dialogue-friendly mix, restrained dynamics, clean soft ending
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ solo felt piano ｜ warm morning room tone]

[Theme A ｜ two-note child motif ｜ muted music box answers]

[Light Motion ｜ soft brushed percussion enters ｜ nylon guitar harmonics ｜ countryside passing by]

[Dialogue Space ｜ instruments thin out ｜ piano stays close ｜ leave room for father and child]

[Transition ｜ music box glints once ｜ gentle stop before old house voices]

[Outro ｜ short piano tail ｜ soft room fade]
```

剪辑点：父亲说“今天元宵节，回老家”时轻入；车窗外城市退远时加入 music box；到老家院门前停一拍，把空间让给爷爷奶奶。

## 05_Cue 2：爷爷的木车

用途：爷爷拿出木车、院子里疯玩、孩子炫耀。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental warm rural childhood cue, 84 BPM, 4/4, playful handmade wooden toy feeling, light kalimba, music box three-note wooden wheel motif, nylon guitar plucks, soft upright bass, tiny wooden percussion like tapping on a workbench, warm yellow old-house ambience, gentle happy movement, natural film score texture
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ tiny wooden taps ｜ warm old-house air]

[Theme A ｜ three-note wooden wheel motif ｜ kalimba and music box]

[Playful Motion ｜ nylon guitar plucks ｜ soft upright bass enters ｜ light forward movement]

[Lift ｜ wooden percussion brighter ｜ motif repeats like wheels rolling downhill]

[Small Break ｜ bass drops out ｜ only wood taps and kalimba]

[Outro ｜ motif lands softly ｜ leave room for the wheel crack sound]
```

剪辑点：木车第一次“咕噜噜”跑起来时，三音母题完整出现；孩子从坡上滑下来时节奏稍微动起来；木车撞坏前突然抽掉低频，只留木头敲击。

## 06_Cue 3：汤圆硬币

用途：包汤圆、上桌、别人吃到硬币、小安越来越急、推倒爷爷。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental tense family dinner cue, 70 BPM, 4/4, warm kitchen felt piano opening, soft porcelain bowl percussion, repeated muted piano notes tightening gradually, subtle low cello drone, light spoon-against-bowl texture, winter festival dinner atmosphere, child anxiety rising, restrained tension, clean sudden break for dramatic silence
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ warm kitchen piano ｜ soft steam-like pad]

[Family Table ｜ bowl percussion ｜ gentle felt piano ｜ calm festival warmth]

[Uneasy Loop ｜ repeated muted piano note ｜ spoon texture begins]

[Build ｜ low cello drone enters ｜ piano repetition tightens ｜ rhythm narrows]

[Peak ｜ porcelain ticks closer ｜ bass pressure rises quietly]

[Break ｜ sudden silence ｜ leave the fall and adult shouts exposed]

[Aftermath ｜ one low piano note ｜ no resolution ｜ fade under blanket silence]
```

剪辑点：奶奶讲“硬币汤圆”时仍然温暖；小安开始戳破汤圆后钢琴单音重复；推开爷爷那一下不要砸大鼓，直接切静音，留下碗汤晃动和大人喊声。

## 07_Cue 4：病床和不懂死亡

用途：乡村医生离开、爷爷输液、小安问“是不是我推的”。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental fragile rural bedroom cue, 62 BPM, sparse felt piano, slowed single-note music box motif, faint bowed vibraphone, soft low strings, long silence between phrases, old wooden room ambience, IV bottle stillness, child guilt and grandfather tenderness, very close intimate mix, restrained emotional weight
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ sparse felt piano ｜ long room silence]

[Stillness ｜ faint bowed vibraphone ｜ soft low string shadow]

[Memory Motif ｜ slowed music box note ｜ piano answers quietly]

[Question Space ｜ instruments hold back ｜ leave room for the child asking]

[Tender Line ｜ low strings warm slightly ｜ motif almost resolves]

[Outro ｜ unresolved piano note ｜ long fade]
```

剪辑点：医生说“老人家底子本来就弱，莫再摔了”后音乐才进；爷爷说“爷爷说话算话”时给一个很轻的木车母题，但不要解决和弦，让它悬着。

## 08_Cue 5：爷爷走了

用途：放学路、村民告知、姐姐跑回去、爷爷遗像、第一次叩首。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental restrained grief cue, 58 BPM, slow low felt piano pulses, distant harmonium-like pad, sparse cello notes, dry rural room ambience, delayed understanding, child POV confusion, school road to funeral house, white cloth and portrait atmosphere, quiet kneeling rhythm, understated documentary feeling
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ environment first ｜ two seconds of air]

[Low Pulse ｜ felt piano single notes ｜ distant pad barely enters]

[Running Away ｜ pulse continues ｜ no full melody ｜ sister disappears ahead]

[Funeral Room ｜ sparse cello note ｜ dry room ambience ｜ white cloth stillness]

[Kneeling ｜ low piano pulse slows ｜ leave room for ritual sound]

[Incomplete Motif ｜ wooden wheel motif first two notes only ｜ third note withheld]

[Outro ｜ air and room tone ｜ fade without resolution]
```

剪辑点：村民说“你们爷爷走了”后先留环境声；姐姐跑远时不要马上大旋律，只给低音脉冲；看到爷爷遗像时出现母题前两个音，第三个音不落下。

## 09_Cue 6：奶奶的叮嘱到遗像硬切

用途：奶奶叠衣服、叮嘱、硬切到奶奶遗像。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental intimate farewell cue, 64 BPM, close felt piano, soft nylon guitar harmonics, barely-there warm pad, slow three-note motif with missing final resolution, quiet rural room, folded clothes, everyday instructions that feel like goodbye, sudden empty cut feeling, restrained intimate film score
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ close felt piano ｜ quiet old clock feeling]

[Grandmother Room ｜ soft nylon harmonics ｜ warm pad underneath]

[Everyday Goodbye ｜ three-note motif begins ｜ final note held back]

[Dialogue Space ｜ piano only ｜ leave room for grandmother instructions]

[Hard Cut ｜ half-beat silence ｜ room tone drops]

[Portrait Return ｜ same motif colder ｜ sparse piano and air]

[Outro ｜ unresolved final note ｜ slow fade]
```

剪辑点：奶奶说“你妈走得早，没人慢慢教你”时只留钢琴；硬切到奶奶遗像时音乐断半拍，再用更空的同一母题回来。

## 10_Cue 7：火光和三件木车相关物

用途：纸房子燃烧、旧话回响、院子散场、院中两辆完整木车和一架半成品木马车架、拨动 P11 悬空后轮、字幕《不想长大》。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental final emotional cue, 52 BPM, very sparse felt piano, slowed music box wooden wheel motif, soft low cello, thin warm strings entering near the final wooden carts, subtle ash and old courtyard ambience, long silences, bittersweet late regret, intimate ending, child hand turning a dusty wheel, final note leaves air
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ low piano and ash-like ambience ｜ long silence]

[Firelight ｜ soft low cello enters ｜ slow breathing space]

[Memory Motif ｜ music box wooden wheel motif very slow ｜ piano answers]

[Old Voices Space ｜ instruments stay low ｜ leave room for remembered lines]

[Final Build ｜ thin warm strings enter ｜ two wooden carts revealed]

[Wheel Moment ｜ strings fall away ｜ solo piano and music box ｜ leave room for wooden wheel sound]

[Final Note ｜ motif completes once ｜ long fade]

[Silence ｜ 2 seconds air for title card]
```

剪辑点：纸房子烧起来时音乐不要冲高；爷爷 VO“爷爷说话算话”后让三音母题完整出现；小安拨动 P11 悬空后轮时，把持续音降下来，只留钢琴、music box 和木轮声；字幕出来后留 2-3 秒尾音和环境声。

## 11_整片主基调参考

如果想先生成一条全片统一气质参考，可以用这一版。

### Style Prompt

```text
Instrumental nostalgic family short film score, rural Chinese childhood memory, bittersweet coming-of-age regret, 72 BPM, 4/4, warm felt piano, soft music box wooden wheel motif, light kalimba, nylon guitar, subtle wooden percussion, faint old-room ambience, restrained strings in emotional peaks, intimate close sound, gentle film grain feeling, child POV, warm old house, winter festival, handmade wooden toy car, late regret, dialogue-friendly mix
```

### Lyrics / Custom Lyrics

```text
[Instrumental Intro ｜ warm felt piano ｜ old-room ambience]

[Theme A ｜ music box wooden wheel motif ｜ light kalimba]

[Warm Memory ｜ nylon guitar enters ｜ soft wooden percussion]

[Tension Turn ｜ piano motif slows ｜ low strings appear]

[Grief Space ｜ sparse piano ｜ long silence between phrases]

[Final Return ｜ wooden wheel motif slower and emptier ｜ thin strings]

[Outro ｜ final piano note fades ｜ leave air]
```

用途：片头、预告、全片基调参考，不一定直接铺完整短剧。

## 12_一句话总控

这套 BGM 的核心不是把观众按哭，而是让观众先听见童年的木轮声，最后再听见它停下来。
