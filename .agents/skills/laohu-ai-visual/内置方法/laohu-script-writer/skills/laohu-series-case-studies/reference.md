从六集样本读出的量化规律：

- **teaser 2–8 页，中位数 5**；与"观众需要多少前情"成反比（premise pilot 8 页最长，双集下半 2 页最短）。
- **总页 58–69**，S1 厚于 S2（68／69 → 67／61／58／58）。
- **幕长两型**：前重后轻仅 Pilot（19／18／12／11）；其余五集后重，四幕最长或次长。二、三幕最易压缩（17 People 10／10），**四幕从不短于 11 页**。
- **故事线 4–7 条，标准 4**；bottle 集 5 条、季终 7 条最多。
- **teaser 出幕 100% 是 `SMASH CUT TO: MAIN TITLES`**；每幕出幕 100% 是 `FADE OUT.` / `FADE TO BLACK` ＋ `END OF ACT N`。格式固定，变化全在内容。
- **每集至少一条"程序线"**（古巴木筏／军事打击／政府连续性／恐袭 FAA／海地使馆）：不承担出幕，提供打断、期限与最终的道德数据。Pilot 四幕的木筏数字：1200 出发／700 折返／350 失踪／137 拘留。

### 1.3 对白范例

**范例 1（S1E01 Pilot 一幕，p0033–p0037）群戏接力句**

```
TOBY  What to do when the Nina, the Pinta, and the Get Me the Hell
      Out Of Here hit Miami.
SAM   We can't send 'em back. They'll go to jail if they're lucky.
TOBY  We'll get whacked in--what--at least--
SAM   --three Congressional districts. Dade County--
TOBY  --those seats are gone.
JOSH  Not to mention the fact that it's wrong.
SAM   Plus that.
```

中文说明：一人起头另一人接完，句子被切成半句；道德判断放在政治算计之后，用两个字 "Plus that." 压住，喜剧与立场同时完成。同场舞台指示写明 "Throughout this scene, LEO will drift in and out of participation... In other words, nothing going on here is an emergency."

**范例 2（S1E01 Pilot 一幕，p0039–p0041）用反常动作代替台词**

```
JOSH  And when was the last time you brought me a cup of coffee?
      (DONNA thinks...)
JOSH  It was never. You've never brought me a cup of coffee.
DONNA Well, if you're gonna make a big deal out of--
JOSH  Donna, if I get fired I get fired.
DONNA Do you think he's gonna do it?
JOSH  (beat) No.
```

中文说明：第一次送咖啡＝"我担心你"；关心由观众自己推出，没有人说出口。

**范例 3（S1E03 二幕 Situation Room，p0113–p0115）同一问句重复三次**

```
BARTLET      What is the virtue of a proportional response?
FITZWALLACE  I'm sorry?
BARTLET      What is the virtue of a proportional response? Why's it good?
             (An awkward silence... They're not geared up for these kinds
             of questions.)
BARTLET      They hit an airplane, so we hit a transmitter, right?
             ... They hit a barracks, we hit two transmitters.
FITZWALLACE  (pause) That's roughly it, yes sir.
```

中文说明：同一问句每次前面加更多铺垫，让军方答不出；答案拖到三幕才由 Fitzwallace 给出——"It isn't virtuous, Mr. President. It's all there is."

**范例 4（S1E03 二幕，p0110–p0111）把对方的攻击原样还回去**

```
SAM   ...you don't have the guts or the strength or the courage to say
      "We know what's right from wrong and this is none of your damn business."
C.J.  Really.
SAM   Yes.
C.J.  Strength, guts or courage.
SAM   Yes.
C.J.  You just said three things that all mean the same thing.
```

中文说明：争吵在修辞层面闭环——不反驳内容，只点出对方的修辞破绽。

**范例 5（S2E02 二幕泳池，p0252–p0255）招募场的四步**

```
TOBY  Jed Bartlet's very impressed with you. He likes the work you did
      with that girls' group with the stupid name.
C.J.  Emily's List?
...
C.J.  He's never heard of me, has he.
TOBY  No.
...
C.J.  How much does it pay?
TOBY  How much were you making before?
C.J.  550,000 a year.
TOBY  This pays six-hundred dollars a week.
C.J.  So this is less!
```

中文说明：吹捧→戳破→报价落差→唯一真正的问题（"Is Jed Bartlet a good man?"）。$550,000／年 对 $600／周 这组数字本身就是人物的决定。

**范例 6（S2E18 二幕，p0322–p0323）把秘密做成可计数的清单**

```
TOBY  How is it possible that this was kept a secret. First of all, who else knows?
LEO   You're the 16th person.
TOBY  Who else?
LEO   I'll tell you some of them, I won't tell you all of them.
...
TOBY  Nobody lied? Is that what you've been saying to yourself over and over again
      for a year?
```

中文说明：知情人数 15→16→17 是观众的进度条，集名就是这个数字（14 号是 Hoynes，15 号是麻醉师 Dr. David Lee）。四幕 Toby 的纠正："It's 17 people by the way. You knew. We weren't counting you."

**范例 7（S2E22 三幕，p0387–p0388）无台词出幕的语法必须先被念出来**

```
DELORES  ...if you think we're right and you won't speak up 'cause you can't
         be bothered, then God, Jed, I don't even want to know you.
         (beat) Come on, I'll get you a triple-A.
JED      Miss Mueller only gets half as much to teach music as Mr. Ryan gets
         to coach crew?
DELORES  (pause) You're gonna do it.
JED      Now I didn't say that.
DELORES  Yes you did.
JED      When?
DELORES  Just then. You stuck your hands in your pockets, you looked away and
         smiled. That means you made up your mind.
...
DELORES  And looked away and smiled. We're in.
```

中文说明：三个构成动作（手插口袋／看向别处／微笑）被角色分开逐项说出，四幕的沉默出幕才可读。

**范例 8（S2E22 四幕 button，p0411–p0412）切黑切在动作半途**

```
BARTLET   Yes. Sandy.
REPORTER  Mr. President, can you tell us right now if you'll be seeking a
          second term?
And BARTLET doesn't answer... the staff is getting curious...
BARTLET   (pause) I'm sorry, Sandy, there was a bit of noise there, could you
          repeat the question.
The staff is thinking "Please, God, come to me papa..."
SANDY     Can you tell us right now if you'll be seeking a second term?
Again BARTLET is silent...
LEO       (to TOBY) Watch this.
And BARTLET thinks... looks away... sticks his left hand in his pocket...
and just as he smiles we FADE TO BLACK.
```

中文说明：三层——角色主动制造延迟、内部人替观众发令（"Watch this."）、在动作完成的半途切黑（"just as he smiles"）。答案从未被说出。

**Chase 前言给出的配方（原话）**："Each script has approximately **thirty-five beats**—**thirteen** or so in the A and B stories, usually **five to six** in the C story, and sometimes a D story with just a few beats." ### 2.3 Pilot 建立了什么 / 刻意不解释什么

**建立**

- 世界：北新泽西；垃圾生意"was"面包黄油；RICO 与告密者；"things are trending downward"；HMO 骗保作为新财路（p0049）。
- 核心家庭：Carmela（豆罐现金、AK-47、Father Phil、"goomar"）、Meadow（15，聪明、撒谎、Aspen）、AJ（13，生日）。
- 工作家庭：Christopher（急于被认可、鲁莽、想把人生卖给好莱坞）、Big Pussy、Paulie、Silvio、Hesh（父亲的犹太顾问）、Dick Barone；Artie／Charmaine 是合法世界的镜子。
- 治疗：Melfi 的规则（听到谋杀要报警，"Technically"）、她的好奇与恐惧、Tony 的抗拒（Gary Cooper）与依赖（"Talking helps"）。
- Livia：拒绝一切善意、"girls take better care of their mothers than sons"、把关心解读成谋杀（"locked away in a nursing home I'll die faster"）。
- Junior：被侄子管着的叔叔，"Something may have to be done"。
- 主题意象：鸭子（飞走＝家庭离散）、大教堂（祖辈用手建造的东西 vs 今天"两个能给浴缸勾缝的都找不到"）、"sad clown"。

**刻意不解释**

- Tony 的正式身份（"The guy who says how things go is who I think I am"——不说 boss/capo，Jackie Aprile 根本没出场）；"waste management"的具体运作（"On this here also you don't need to know the details"）。
- Christopher 与 Tony 的确切亲缘（既说 nephew 又说 "my cousin Anthony"）；Christopher 的毒瘾只由 Paulie 一句带过。
- 谁炸了 Vesuvio——观众看到 Silvio 走开，Artie 不知道，**剧中没有任何人解释**。
- Junior 的"New York"结构（"He's got their okay on the hit"）；Tony 父亲怎么死的。
- Junior 与 Livia 的默契会导致什么——完全留白。
- Meadow 到底知道多少（她的 "cynical look"）——留到 College。
- 被删的两场（Tony 吻 Melfi、Meadow 的火刑幻想）说明 pilot 也在试错：过早的关系推进与过度风格化的幻想都被剪掉，**但剧本集保留了这两场**，可对照。

### 2.4 范例

**范例 1（Pilot，p0025–p0026, sc.18）医患共谋的建立**

```
MELFI: ...if I was, for example, to hear that a... say a... murder?...
       was about to take place—
       ...I'm supposed to go to the authorities. Technically.
TONY (long beat): Oh.
MELFI: I don't know what happened with this Mahaffey fellow. I'm just saying.
TONY: I see. (beat) Nothing. We had coffee.
```

中文说明：观众刚看完撞人打人；"We had coffee." 是双重生活的反讽公式，此后每次治疗都建立在这条"可说／不可说"的边界上。

**范例 2（Pilot，p0046, sc.44）抗拒与承认在同一场完成**

```
TONY: Whatever happened to Gary Cooper? The strong silent type. That was an
American. He wasn't in touch with his feelings. He just did what he had to
do... once they got Gary Cooper in touch with his feelings, they wouldn't be
able to shut him up! Dysfunction this! Dysfunction that!
MELFI: Do you feel depressed?
```

### 3.3 Fellowes 创作法则（从 419 条脚注提炼）

1. **"Downton dilemma＝两边都对"**（脚注里出现最多的一条）："a typical Downton argument, where hopefully you're on both sides."／"A Downton dilemma is when you see both sides." 用法：Robert vs Cora 该不该送 Matthew 回家；Clarkson 该不该为大宅破例；Ethel 该不该交出孩子；Daisy 该不该对临终的人说真话；Strallan 的自我否决 vs Edith 的渴望。
2. **没有完全不可同情的角色**："There are no entirely unsympathetic characters in Downton. But there is always a sub-text, or at least I try to suggest one."（O'Brien 的赎罪；Bryant "afraid of his own grief"；Carlisle "he always tells the truth with Mary"。）
3. **每集开头用两三句台词复述在推进的线**："never assume that they will remember every detail of an earlier episode, because they won't."（例：Ep7 Sc2–3 用"现在该叫他 Mr 还是 Captain"与"Bates 的事有消息吗"各勾一条。）
4. **群戏规格（脚注 326）**："the luncheons, the dinners, the drawing-room scenes… they always have to **advance about six or seven stories at the same time, often by no more than a line**. So the audience has to store it all and then put the plots together at the end."
5. **让观众比角色知道得多**："you engineer uncomfortable meetings, and then you let the audience fill in the detail."／"the audience always likes knowing things that the people on screen don't know."
6. **观众知道了就不必让他们看见**：Matthew 听真相不给台词；性爱与暴力让观众自行想象，"owes more to old Hollywood than modern television"。
7. **上楼与下楼的情感故事要等重**："giving them both the same attention"（Ep5 的 Mary/Matthew 与 Daisy/William 平行），并用镜像对照让同一件事在两种语域里各说一次（Carson/Hughes 对 Violet/Isobel）。
8. **刻意制造本来不会相遇的两人的场**："there is no greater distance at Downton than that between the Dowager Countess and Daisy"——为此允许一点制度脱序（白天添火本该是男仆的活）。
9. **调度技法**：晚餐散席全体起立可重组人物配对；打猎一天的流程（走到第一轮、中途喝汤、谷仓午餐）"can bring characters together or push them apart"；走廊上的 passage acting 用来并行几条线。
10. **workplace drama 的留人前提**："One of the basic questions of any drama is: why do these men and women stay?"——Downton 的答案是"这是他们的生计"，所以每隔一段要提醒观众这是个有预算、有工资、有职级的工作场所。
11. **"truth reminders"**：每隔一段埋一句刺破温情的真话（主人不知道厨娘的名字："Who is Daisy?"），让群像不至于变成 love-in。
12. **杀角色的规则**："it wouldn't be right if no one from our running cast died"（脚注 120，三位制片反复讨论）；死者必须观众熟悉且喜爱——"It's like a whodunit. The killer has to be someone who was there all the time."；并**故意误导观众猜 Cora**（还顺带让人去想"给 Robert 找新妻子生儿子"的剧情可能）。
13. **季末必须给回来的理由**：最后一幕是纯 pay-off ＋ "streamers"——"we won't resolve this tonight, but if you keep watching, at some point we will."
14. **恋人合体的时机判断**："Any writer or producer of a series always dreads allowing lovers finally to come together… So you keep them apart and keep them apart, but… there comes a time when the audience thinks, oh, for Christ's sake, are they going to get together or not?"
15. **戏剧要求的逻辑高于事实的逻辑**："drama requires a logic not always supplied by truth."——但史实与医学必须查实（Decree Nisi 因 collusion 被撤销、spinal shock vs transection、brachial plexus、Spanish flu 专杀青年），并且偏好"看起来费解、查了却完全准确"的引用。脚注 306：Strallan 的伤是设计出来的——要严重但不需特效（不能缺肢，太贵太麻烦），所以请医学顾问给了 brachial plexus；这段解释又被剪，因为它"undermined the truth of the situation and turned it into a sort of stage convention"。
16. **不给时代剧人物现代态度**："It always slightly annoys me when characters in period drama, in order to make them sympathetic, are given modern attitudes and modern choices."（Sybil 的婚前贞操观与她的政治立场并不冲突。）
17. **不解释就放进去**："the style of Downton… refer to people and events… without making a point of explaining them."
18. **制作现实会改写剧本**：Highclere 地毯卷起要房主批准；餐厅场次有上限（Carson 与男仆站不住）；常备一间可改装的无天花板摄影棚卧室（可移墙、因此能俯拍）；演员档期→删场或写出离场；**演员演得够好就删掉对白**（Thomas 的动机、Jane 的台词）——"A good actor's skill will always obviate some of the dialogue."
19. **剪辑是三人合议，作者常输并承认自己错**："the consensus went against me… I think I was wrong and they were right."（Vera 陈尸作结尾）；丢掉的多半是解释性台词与"新颜色"的小动作，作者每次都记下损失了什么。
20. **美国播出无"特辑"概念**：圣诞特辑必须"play either as the end of a series, or as a stand-alone"。
21. **角色的对立面要写成"制度"而不是"恶意"**：Bryant 要书面证据、私生子在法律上对父亲无请求权、Carlisle 买断丑闻、Decree Nisi 被撤销——阻力由规则提供，反派只是最懂规则的人。
22. **笑点与权力同一句话**：Dowager 的台词常同时是 scene button 与权力行使（"I hope it is not vulgar in me to suggest that you find some way to overcome your scruples."／"I have a cold."）。

**四种 act out（全季只用这四种）**：(a) 一个人越界提出请求（Carlisle 收买 Anna）；(b) 一个隐藏事实被一句话说出（"He is standing."）；(c) Dowager 式的一句按钮（"Worse luck."／"I have a cold."）；(d) 一个身体事实（尸体、手铐、崩溃大哭、登台读经）。

中文说明：先给看的人的视线，再给原因；揭示由舞台指示完成（"He is standing."），角色一个字都不解释。紧接的群像反应场先让全家误以为是别的事（他又坐回椅子上，"a slight let-down"）做缓冲。

**范例 5（圣诞特辑 Sc69、Sc110）不让女主把过失浪漫化／用"我不原谅你"表达原谅**

```
MARY: It was lust, Matthew, or a need for excitement or something in him
that I — Oh, God, what difference does it make? I'm Tess of the
d'Urbervilles to your Angel Clare. I have fallen. I am impure.
MATTHEW: Don't joke. Don't make it little. Not when I'm trying to understand.
...（Sc110）
MATTHEW: I haven't forgiven you because I don't believe you need my
forgiveness. You've lived your life and I've lived mine. And now it's time
we lived them together.
MARY: You must say it properly. I won't answer unless you kneel down and
everything.
```

中文说明：他要原谅的必须是真正发生过的事，不是他愿意听的版本；而最大规格的成全（雪、下跪）之后，回答被压到最短。

## 四、Fleabag：两季 12 集表与范例

来源：Phoebe Waller-Bridge《Fleabag: The Scriptures》（Sceptre, 2019），两季 12 集拍摄稿（每集约 25 分钟）＋卷首短序＋卷末〈Who is Fleabag?〉与〈Post-script〉（约 190 行，按合作者分节）。电子版无页码，定位用 txt 行号 `L`。**半小时剧无 ACT 标记——下表的"段落"划分是按地点与时间块的推断；书面事实是固定的段落装置与 S1 每集剧本约 2900–3600 行。**

每集骨架（两季严格执行）：`cold open（1–3 分钟，自带一个笑点或一个谜）→ TITLES: FLEABAG → 3–7 段 → button（≤1 页，常无台词）`。**唯一例外 S2E6** 用 `TITLE CARD: THE WEDDING` 替代片头。

### 4.1 12 集弧表（核心问题／装置用法／关系状态）

| 集 | 核心问题 | 装置（to camera）怎么用 | 关系状态（镜头／Claire／爱情线／亡者） |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1E1 | 她能不能开口求助？ | 同谋模式建立：`You know that feeling when…` 开场；每个新人物配一句 to camera 定性句（Dad、Claire、Godmother） | 镜头是唯一知情者；Claire 抢先拒绝借钱；Arsehole Guy＝工具；Boo 之死被当笑话讲给出租车司机 |
| S1E2 | Harry 走后，她用什么填空？ | 教学式旁白（"And you find yourself saying things like–"）＋一拍反转一拍泄气 | Harry 线关闭（"This time really is forever."）；Hilary（仓鼠）作为 Boo 的替身进场 |
| S1E3 | 谁真的认识谁？（Claire 的生日） | 信息经济：母亲的死、双乳切除用一段 to camera 交代完；同一问题问两组人 | 她替姐姐背下"Martin 之吻"的秘密；Jack 登场＝季弧第一次露底；她第一次主动抱 Hilary |
| S1E4 | 不许说话的地方，能不能说真话？ | 沉默集里镜头成为她唯一的"声音"；"Let go of your past." / "(to camera) Bit on the nose." | 对 Claire 说真话→被抛下（只留两颗电池）；Bank Manager＝唯一能交换真话的人；Boo 的语音信箱出现 |
| S1E5 | Claire 会不会为她出头？ | 进门前对镜头立规则，规则随即应验（预言即笑点） | 伪胜利最高点：Claire 要离开 Martin、给钱、去芬兰；雕像被归还 |

| S2E1 | "This is a love story"——谁的？ | in medias res（cold open＝结局倒数第二格）；同桌者随她的视线逐个揭示 | Priest 登场＝"a man she couldn't dismiss"；一年隔阂靠"The priest was quite hot."一句修复；她替姐姐认领流产 |
| S2E2 | 她想要的是神父还是上帝？ | 实况判断（律师楼"They've definitely fucked."连环更正）；"别说—说了"（五拍三次转头） | Priest 给她标了页的圣经；咨询师替她命名 want（"Do you want to fuck the priest or do you want to fuck God?"） |
| S2E3 | "We're not friends. We are sisters." | **反转二**：Priest 第一次看见装置——"What was that? Where did you just go?" | 姐妹第一次互相说狠的真话；Belinda 给出"prime/menopause"独白；雕像变成奖杯又回到她手里 |
| S2E4 | 她能不能不表演地把真话交出去？ | `(accidentally to camera)` / `(accidentally to Priest)` 误说；她再次躲镜头并奔跑；告解里是全剧**唯一一段不对镜头的长独白** | 告解→"Kneel."→吻→耶稣画像落地→他逃；母亲葬礼闪回命名了她的 need："I don't know where to put all the love I have for her." |
| S2E5 | 为了他，她愿意清空多少自己？ | **反转三**："FOR FUCK'S SAKE STOP THAT…"；结尾她**把镜头推开** | 上床；她第一次把自己的事让位给 Claire（头发、流产、Klare、巴松管）；Martin 崩塌（"Don't let her leave me."） |

### 4.2 两季的对称结构（六集季可直接照抄的骨架）

| 位置 | Series One | Series Two | 功能 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第 1 集 | 一天内把全部人物＋欠钱＋Boo 之谜铺开（typical episode pilot，**不是 premise pilot**） | 一场订婚晚宴（单一地点）把全部人物＋新人物＋全季悬念铺开 | 引擎展示：**人物关系网本身就是引擎**，不靠事件 |
| 第 2 集 | 前任回收（Harry）＋Hilary 进场 | 教堂／咖啡（两人独处）＋咨询室 | 把第 1 集的情绪换成一个可追的欲望 |
| 第 3 集 | 家庭场合（Claire 生日）＋季弧露底 | 家庭场合（颁奖礼）＋装置第一次被看见 | 第 3 集放第一次"底牌" |
| 第 4 集 | 瓶子集（静修营：封闭空间、禁语） | 瓶子集（告解室：封闭空间、规则游戏） | 脱离常规地点的双人深潜，单集主题最纯 |
| 第 5 集 | 伪胜利最高点（姐妹同盟、拿到钱） | 伪胜利最高点（上床、姐妹和解） | 给"最好的一天"，为第 6 集的崩塌蓄力 |
| 第 6 集 | 全员召回一个空间（Sexhibition）＋真相＋镜头追她 | 全员召回一个空间（婚礼）＋全部物件结清＋她遣散镜头 | 季终＝一个封闭的社交场合，每条线在此关门 |

季终集的 button 都不是情节：S1E6 是"People make mistakes."＋铅笔橡皮；S2E6 是狐狸、雕像、挥手。**半小时剧的最后一个画面解决主题，不解决情节。**

卷末评注里的四条原句（写作判据）：

- 装置通过测试的那一刻（Vicky Jones, L43484）："When trying to crack the pilot episode, Vicky filmed me on her phone as I walked around my kitchen. I was making tea and experimenting talking to the camera. After five minutes she pulled the phone down and grinned: 'It works.'"
- Visual Sentences（Harry Bradbeer, L43507）："Harry described the image of Boo standing across the street with cars zooming past her. He said that was all we needed. … She has such a formidable armour of wit and self-awareness, but drop that in at any point – her laughing at a party, her during sex, her walking down the street feeling 'great' – and we'd know that she isn't OK."
- 续季的唯一判据（L43511）："'If she has something else to learn, then we have more story to tell'. Harry was convinced that the greatest love story we had to tell was that between Fleabag and herself."
- 演员的否决权与加词（Sian Clifford, L43537；Andrew Scott, L43562–43564）："There were a few times I tried to change the lines on set, and she would quietly stop me. 'It's good. Stop it.'"／"He even insisted on the character saying 'I love you too' to Fleabag at the end, and thank God he did."

### 4.3 范例

**范例 1（S1E4 静修营，约 L11900）配角交出 want 清单，主角只回一句**

```
BANK MANAGER  They keep asking me. What do you want from this? What do you
              want? (beat) I'm not telling them what I want ...
Beat. A beautiful track starts playing quietly under his next dialogue.
BANK MANAGER  I want to move back home, I want to hug my wife, I want to
              protect my children ... I want to take clean cups out of the
              dishwasher ... and put them in the cupboard ... At home. And
              the next morning I want to watch my wife drink from them.
He smiles. She smiles. Pause.
FLEABAG       I just want to cry ... all the time.
He looks at her. His smile falls slightly. ... She zips her mouth shut.
He zips her mouth shut. They both look out.
```

中文说明：配角的 want 从大到小（家→杯子），主角只用六个字；音乐提示写进剧本。

**范例 2（S1E5 餐桌，L14900 起）同一问题问两组人**

```
GODMOTHER  Gooooosh! You really are ... So how did you two meet?
FLEABAG (to camera)  Fucked me up the arse.
ARSEHOLE GUY  I used to manage a bar, and I just found her, crying in the
              toilet one night.
Pause.
ARSEHOLE GUY (to Dad and Godmother)  How about you two? How did you two meet?
FLEABAG    Through our mother actually.
Beat.
DAD        How's work, Claire?
```

中文说明：to camera 版本与"官方"版本并置；Dad 换话题＝逃避的动作。同场的门厅耳光遵守"礼貌即暴力"：最狠的一句用 "(with a gentle curiosity)" 说出，暴力之后立刻回到礼貌表演（"Oh whoops. Look at these."）。

**范例 3（S1E6 服务间，L18800 起）装置反转一：镜头追她**

```
Beat. Fleabag's breathing picks up. She looks at the camera.
INT. FLASHBACK, FLEABAG'S BEDROOM – NIGHT
Close-up on hands undoing a man's belt buckle.
INT. SERVICE AREA – EVENING
Fleabag turns away from Claire and Martin. She tries to avoid the camera,
then turns back and looks straight into it, glancing between the camera
and Claire.
...
Fleabag turns and tries to back away from the camera but it follows her as
she backs into the coat rail.
```

中文说明：季弧悬念（Boo 之死的真相）被拆成 2 行一段的闪回碎片埋了三集，在这里由**装置本身**逼出；观众第一次从同谋变成审问者。

**范例 4（S2E3 花园，L32000–32845）装置第一次被剧中人看见**

```
PRIEST   I'd really like to be your friend though.
FLEABAG  I'd like to be your friend too.
         (to camera) We will last a week.
PRIEST   What was that?
FLEABAG  What?
PRIEST   Where did ... Where did you just go?
FLEABAG  What?
PRIEST   You just went somewhere.
Fleabag looks at the camera in panic.
PRIEST   There. There. Where did you just go?
FLEABAG  Nowhere.
```

中文说明：S1 的叙事装置在此变成剧情事件；"panic" 是写给镜头的，观众成了被捉的一方。

**范例 5（S2E4 告解室，L35811–36456）全剧唯一一段不对镜头的长独白**

```
FLEABAG  No, I want someone to tell me what to wear every morning … I want
         someone to tell me what to eat, what to like, what to hate, what to
         rage about, what to listen to, what band to like, what to buy tickets
         for, what to joke about, what not to joke about. I want someone to
         tell me what to believe in, who to vote for, who to love and how to …
         tell them.
         (beat)
         I just think I want someone to tell me how to live my life, Father,
         because so far, I think I've been getting it wrong. … Just fucking
         tell me what to do, Father.
Silence. She looks to the grate. She can't make out his expression. A long pause.
PRIEST (O.S.)  Kneel.
FLEABAG  What?
PRIEST (O.S.)  Kneel. … (gently) Just kneel.
```

中文说明：十二个 what/who 的清单式排比把"我不知道怎么活"说成喜剧节奏；对方只用一个词回答，而这个词同时是宗教指令与情欲指令；格栅替代了反应镜头。她看不见他的表情，观众也看不见。

**范例 6（S2E6 公交站 button，L43118–43434）anti-climax 七拍收尾**

```
"You nailed it." → 显示屏 46 分钟 "They always lie. It'll magically come in a
minute." → "It's God, isn't it?" "Yeah." → "That I fucking love you. I love
you." → "No no don't, let's just leave that out there for a second on its
own." → "I love you." → 他牵她手 "It'll pass." → 他走 → "I love you too.
(beat) Ok." → 公交显示 Cancelled → 狐狸经过 → "He went that way." → 她拿出
雕像 → 起身走，镜头跟了两步 → 她回头，几乎看不出地摇头，请我们不要跟 → 走远处
回头挥手 → "Goodbye." / THE END
```

中文说明：表白被允许说三次（第二次由她自己要求"让它单独待一会儿"），回答只有两个字；结尾不靠台词而靠一个**摇头**完成装置的告别，全剧最后一句台词是写给镜头的舞台指示 "Goodbye."。
